## Company Context

H.B. Fuller is a global adhesives manufacturer specializing in adhesives, sealants, and specialty chemical solutions that enable product assembly, performance, and durability across multiple industries. Founded more than 130 years ago, the company develops advanced bonding technologies that support modern manufacturing and help improve products and everyday life.

The company’s innovation programs combine materials science, application engineering, and global technical support to solve complex bonding challenges for manufacturers worldwide.

Core areas of expertise include:

* Hot melt adhesive technologies for high-speed manufacturing
* Reactive polyurethane and structural bonding systems
* Adhesive solutions for electric vehicle battery packs and energy storage systems
* Sustainable packaging adhesives designed to support recyclability and circular packaging systems
* Electronics bonding and protection technologies
* Industrial assembly and manufacturing adhesives
* Hygiene and nonwoven product construction adhesives
* Construction sealants and specialty bonding systems

H.B. Fuller serves customers across industries including packaging, hygiene, electronics, medical, transportation, construction, woodworking, clean energy, and general industrial manufacturing.

## Core Adhesive Categories

H.B. Fuller develops adhesive and sealant technologies across multiple chemical and application categories used in industrial manufacturing, packaging, electronics, mobility, hygiene, and construction.

Major adhesive categories include:

- Hot Melt Adhesives (HMA): Thermoplastic adhesives used for high-speed manufacturing, packaging, hygiene product assembly, and product construction.
- Reactive Polyurethane Adhesives (PUR): Moisture-reactive systems providing high strength, durability, and resistance for woodworking, construction, and industrial assembly.
- Water-Based Adhesives: Waterborne adhesive systems used in packaging, labeling, paper converting, and product assembly.
- Pressure-Sensitive Adhesives (PSA): Adhesives designed to maintain tack and adhesion under light pressure for tapes, labels, medical products, and specialty applications.
- Structural Adhesives: High-strength bonding systems used in transportation, electronics, construction, and industrial assembly where mechanical durability is critical.
- Sealants: Flexible sealing materials used in construction, transportation, electronics protection, and industrial applications.
